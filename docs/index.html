HomePlanner

HomePlanner is a family wall calendar for a touch screen in your home (an ESP32-P4 panel). It shows your family's Google Calendar for the week, lets anyone at home add and change events from the wall, and shows the weather.

What it does with your Google account

  • Shows and changes the events of the calendar you choose (your "Family" calendar) on the panel.
  • Lists your calendars, so you can choose the Family calendar and, if you like, show other calendars (for example a school calendar) on the panel.
  • Shows your email address on the panel's settings page, so you know which account is connected.

Your calendar data goes only between Google and the panel in your home. There is no HomePlanner server, no tracking, and nothing is shared with anyone. Read the privacy policy.
